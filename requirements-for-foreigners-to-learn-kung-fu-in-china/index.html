Requirements for Foreigners to Learn Kung Fu in China

For foreigners interested in studying authentic Chinese martial arts, here are the key requirements and considerations:

Basic Requirements

  1. Valid Documentation
  • Passport with appropriate Chinese visa (usually student visa X1/X2)
  • require health certificate and insurance
  • For long-term study: residence permit application
  1. Physical Condition
  • Good health with no serious medical conditions
  • Age requirements vary (typically 6-70 for most schools)
  • Basic physical fitness – training can be demanding
  1. Financial Preparation
  • Tuition fees: $300-800/month depending on school and program
  • Accommodation: $200-500/month (many schools offer dormitories)
  • Additional costs: equipment, uniform, insurance, and personal expenses

Training Options

Formal Martial Arts Schools

  • Registered institutions with certified instructors
  • Structured curriculum with certified training programs
  • Often offer visa assistance for longer courses

University Programs

  • Some sports universities offer martial arts degrees
  • More academic approach combined with physical training
  • Usually require Chinese language proficiency

Temple Training (Shaolin, Wudang)

  • More traditional and rigorous
  • Often include cultural and philosophical education
  • Typically require longer commitment

Practical Considerations

  1. Language Barrier
  • Basic Mandarin helpful but not always required
  • Many schools have English-speaking instructors
  • Learning basic Chinese enhances the experience
  1. Cultural Adaptation
  • Respect for Chinese customs and training etiquette
  • Adaptability to different living conditions
  • Openness to traditional teaching methods
  1. Training Commitment
  • Typical daily training: 4-6 hours
  • Minimum recommended stay: 1 month for basics, 3+ months for significant progress
  • Prepare for physical and mental challenges

Application Process

  1. Research and select preferred school/style
  2. Contact school for requirements and availability
  3. Submit application and required documents
  4. Arrange payment and accommodation
  5. Obtain proper visa
  6. Plan arrival and orientation

Recommended Preparation

  • Basic physical conditioning before arrival
  • Research about specific martial arts styles
  • Learn basic Mandarin phrases
  • Bring appropriate clothing and training shoes
  • Maintain open mindset and patience

Shaolin Temple Center China warmly welcomes all sincere enthusiasts of Zen martial arts culture from around the world to learn and experience its traditions. Whether you are a child seeking physical fitness, an adult pursuing inner peace, or an international friend wishing to delve deeper into Chinese traditional culture,

as long as you approach with sincerity, passion, and respect, everyone, regardless of age, gender, or origin, can find their own unique rewards at the Shaolin Temple Cultural Center China.
