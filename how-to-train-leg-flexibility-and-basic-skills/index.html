How to Train Leg Flexibility and Basic Skills

Published January 6, 2025 · 5 min read

The Importance of Train Leg Flexibility and Basic Skills

Leg strength and Basic Skills are essential in many sports and daily activities. Both play a pivotal role, whether you are pursuing physical fitness, improving your athletic performance, or aiming to increase your endurance and coordination.

Importance of leg strength

  1. Strength training increases the mass of leg muscles and helps to maintain a healthy condition.
  2. Strengthening the legs can improve the efficiency of daily activities such as running, jumping, climbing stairs, etc. and reduce the burden on the body.
  3. Many movements in sports require strong legs for support, such as basketball and soccer, and leg stability and explosive power are key to these sports.

The Importance of Fundamentals

Fundamentals are the foundation of any sport or skill learned. It includes posture, balance, coordination, and accuracy of movement.

  1. good basic function helps to develop correct movement patterns and avoid sports injuries.
  2. Exercising basic functions enhances body control and coordination, thus improving technique.
  3. Improving efficiency and accuracy of basic skills leads to faster mastery of complex techniques.

Pressing the leg is to put the foot on the ground, balance beam, table and stool surface, wall, window sill, etc., the upper body forward leaning downward pressure, the practitioner through the leg pressure to elongate the leg ligaments, muscles, tendons, etc., for the practice of the leg method to lay a good foundation.

  • High Pressing Legs [Practice] Place your legs on an object that is the same height as your waist or the same height as your shoulders, hook your toes up, sit back with your hips, hold your knees with both palms down, lift your chest and close your abdomen, straighten your back and collapse your waist, and touch your toes with your forehead. You can also hold the toes with both hands, and use your forehead to force the collision. Touching the toes can also stop a little. According to the gradual lengthening of the leg ligaments, the forehead touches the foot and stays longer. After the forehead touches, use the chin to close to the toes, and then touch the toes with the jaw. The left and right feet are practiced interchangeably.
  • Low-pressure leg right toe out, full foot landing, right knee bent; left foot steps forward and up, heel on the ground, toes hooked, knee fully extended, sit back on the hip. The left palm stands under the right ribs, the right palm is on the shelf, the left shoulder is in front, the upper body rhythmically vibrates and presses forward and downward, touching the left toe with the head or the right hand. Practice alternating left and right legs. It can also be practiced by letting the soles of the feet rest against steps or stakes, etc.
  • Servant’s Step Leg Press [Practice] One leg bends at the knee and squats, the other leg straightens to the side, the toes buckle in and the whole foot lands on the ground; both hands grasp the outside of the two feet respectively, rhythmically increase the pressure downward for several times, and then turn to press the other leg. Alternate between the right and left legs.

Kicking is the leg straight from the front, side, back, etc. to the head, is a way to lengthen the muscles and ligaments, often practiced can make the leg light foot fast, for a variety of leg method to lay a good foundation.

  • Positive Kick [Exercise] Step forward with the left leg, hook the right toe to kick the forehead. The right foot drops to the front of the body and the toes point to the ground by the rebound force of the leg to the limit. Step forward again and kick your left foot in front of your forehead. Can also be practiced in place, the right foot kicked after the fall behind the body, and then on the right foot to kick the left foot, such as the above repeatedly practiced. You can also do a leg kick 10 times in place, alternating between the two legs to practice.
  • Side Kick Leg Right foot steps forward and forward on the left side of the body, left toe hooks up and kicks fiercely over the head, the right palm rests on the head and the left palm stands under the right armpit. The left foot falls in front of the body, toes pointing to the ground, then step on the right, turn the body to the right 180 degrees, left toe hooked to the head kick. Repeat as above. In-situ practice is slightly the same as the front kick, but first turn the body after the side kick.
  • Lihe leg [practice] left foot forward step, right toe buckle to the left upward kick, through the front to the left side of the upper straight leg Rihe, landing foot in front of the left foot outside, the body slightly right turn. Then, the left foot steps forward, the right toe buckle to the right upward kick, through the front to the left side of the upper straight leg Lihe, landing on the left foot in front of the outside, such as the above repeated exercises.
  • Outside the leg [practice] left foot forward step, right toe straight, kick up to the right side, through the front to the left side of the upper external pendulum, straight leg landing in the left foot. Then, step forward with the right foot, straighten the left toe, kick up to the right side, swing outward to the upper left side in front of you, and drop the straight leg next to the right foot.

Both leg strength exercise and basic skills practice require persistence and the right approach. Through a reasonable training program, combined with the methods and suggestions mentioned above, we can effectively improve our leg strength and basic skills, and lay a solid foundation for better sports performance and healthy living. Remember, persistence is the key to success!
