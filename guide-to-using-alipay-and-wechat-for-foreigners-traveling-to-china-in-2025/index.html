Guide to using Alipay and WeChat for foreigners traveling to China in 2025

In today’s era of rapid digitalization, China’s mobile payment sector is leading the global change in payment methods with its convenience, efficiency and wide coverage. Alipay and WeChat Pay, as two of China’s mobile payment giants, have not only dominated the domestic market, but have also gradually become indispensable payment tools for global tourists and expatriates in China. From shopping malls in bustling cities to kiosks in remote villages, from online shopping platforms to offline dining and entertainment, Alipay and WeChat Pay cover almost all consumer scenarios, revolutionizing the traditional way people pay in cash.

For expats, using mobile payments in China not only means no need to carry large amounts of cash, reducing the risk of theft or loss, but also enjoying unprecedented payment convenience. Both platforms support international bank card binding, making it easy for tourists and learners of different nationalities to access this payment system. In addition, as technology continues to advance, both Alipay and WeChat Pay provide multi-language switching capabilities, ensuring users can operate without barriers no matter where they come from. Continued optimization of the cross-border consumer experience, such as automatic exchange rate conversion and international tax refund services, further enhances the expat experience.

How to use Alipay or WeChat wallet for foreigners

Alipay User Guide

1.1 Registration and Authentication
  • Download and Installation: Download the Alipay APP through major application stores or official websites in China and complete the installation according to the prompts.
  • Register for an account: Register for an account using your email address or cell phone number and follow the process for identity verification. For foreigners, you can choose to authenticate your real name through your passport information.
  • Binding bank card: Support international mainstream bank card binding, including Visa, Mastercard, etc., after completing the binding, you can recharge or directly deduct money from the bank card.
1.2 Features
  • Scan Code Payment: In offline consumption scenarios such as supermarkets, restaurants, etc., complete the payment by scanning the QR code provided by the merchant.
  • Transfer and Collection: Support transferring money to friends or merchants, and generating personal collection code for others to scan for payment.
  • Life Services: covers convenient services such as water, electricity and coal payment, traffic fines, hospital registration, etc., which greatly facilitates daily life.
  • Travel Assistant: Provides travel-related services such as exchange rate inquiries, purchase of overseas Internet traffic packages, and reservation of attraction tickets.
1.3 Security Settings
  • Payment Password: Set and memorize the payment password to ensure transaction security.
  • Fingerprint Identification/Facial Recognition: Enable biometric function to increase payment security.
  • Account Insurance: Consider purchasing account security insurance to provide additional protection for fund security.

WeChat Payment User Guide

1.1 Registration and Binding
  • Download and Installation: Also download and install WeChat APP through app stores or official websites.
  • Registration and Real-Name Authentication: Register a WeChat account using your cell phone number and complete real-name authentication by binding your bank card or passport.
  • Wallet Settings: In WeChat’s “Me”-“Wallet”, set the payment password and bind an international bank card.
1.2 Core Functions
  • Scan code payment: similar to Alipay, complete the payment by scanning the merchant’s QR code.
  • Red Envelope and Transfer: WeChat’s unique Red Envelope function is suitable for holiday celebrations or money exchanges between friends. At the same time, it supports transferring money to individuals or businesses.
  • Small Program Services: WeChat Small Programs provide a wealth of online services, such as hotel booking, movie ticket purchase, etc., which can be used without downloading additional APP.
  • Social Payment: Combined with the social attributes of WeChat, payment requests can be initiated directly in the circle of friends and group chats to enhance social interaction.
1.3 Security Measures
  • Payment password protection: Set complex and unique payment password to avoid leakage.
  • Double Verification: Enable SMS verification or the double verification function provided by WeChat Security Center to enhance account security.
  • Transaction monitoring: Regularly check the account transaction records to detect and handle abnormal transactions in a timely manner.

Tips and Precautions for Use

1.1 Language Switching and Internationalization Services
  • Both Alipay and WeChat Pay support multi-language interfaces, and users can choose English or other languages for operation as needed.
  • When using international services, pay attention to the cross-border payment guide provided by the platform to understand the exchange rate, handling fees and other relevant information.
1.2 Offline Payment and Network Connectivity
  • Although mobile payment relies on the network, both Alipay and WeChat Pay support offline payment function, which allows small amount payment to be completed when the network is unstable or unable to connect.
  • It is recommended to check whether the APP version is up-to-date before traveling to ensure that all functions work properly.
1.3 Solutions when encountering problems
  • When encountering payment problems, you can first try to seek answers through the online customer service or help center in the APP.
  • If the problem is still not solved, you can call the official customer service hotline of Alipay or WeChat Pay to seek human service.
  • Take care to keep transaction records, screenshots and other evidence so that you can file a complaint or appeal if necessary.

Summary and Prospects

With the continuous development of China’s economy and the deepening of opening up to the outside world, mobile payment has become one of the bridges connecting China and the world. Alipay and WeChat Pay have won wide recognition from global users for their convenience, security and internationalized services. For expatriates, mastering these two payment methods will greatly enhance the quality of study and life in China. In the future, as technology continues to advance and application scenarios continue to expand, we have reason to believe that China mobile payment will bring even more colorful payment experiences to global tourists and expatriates in China. Whether you are exploring ancient cultural relics or savoring authentic cuisine, Alipay and WeChat Pay will accompany you in every exciting moment.
