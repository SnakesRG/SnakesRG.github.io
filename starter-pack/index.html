到

Day 1: arrival

  • Free pick-up at Handan train station, included in the application fee. Send your train number and arrival time in advance.

    confirmed

  • Have your passport ready: the school registers you and helps with visa paperwork.

    confirmed

  • Training and living fees are paid on arrival, in cash (USD, RMB, EUR or GBP).

    confirmed

  • Install WeChat and Alipay before you leave home and link a card: cash is rarely used in China.

    general advice
水

Drinking water

  • Drinking water is available in the kitchen of the dining hall, where we take our meals.

    confirmed

  • Do not drink tap water without boiling it.

    general advice

  • Bring a reusable bottle (1 litre or more) and refill it at every meal.

    to be confirmed
食

Meals

  • 3 meals a day, 7 days a week, in the dining hall. Traditional Chinese style: dishes shared by 4 to 6 students per table, with rice and steamed bread.

    confirmed

  • Breakfast: cakes, boiled eggs, fruit and milk powder. Vegetarian meals are available on request.

    confirmed

  • Usual times: breakfast 7:30, lunch 11:30, dinner 18:00.

    to be confirmed

  • Four big feasts a year: Chinese New Year, Mid-Autumn Festival, the academy anniversary and Christmas.

    confirmed
净

Rubbish and recycling

  • Bins are located near the dormitory entrance and next to the dining hall.

    to be confirmed

  • Sort your waste: recyclables, food waste and other waste.

    to be confirmed

  • Keep your room clean and take your rubbish out every day.

    to be confirmed
洗

Laundry

  • Washing machines are available 24 hours a day, free, as often as you need.

    confirmed

  • Washing powder is not provided: buy it at the village shop.

    to be confirmed

  • Drying: use the drying racks on the dormitory balcony or courtyard.

    to be confirmed
买

Shopping

  • Small shops and local restaurants are within walking distance of the school.

    confirmed

  • Not included in the fees: towel, toiletries, washing powder, snacks and drinks. Budget a little for these.

    confirmed

  • For bigger shopping, go to She Xian town with other students (with the masters' permission).

    to be confirmed

  • Most shops accept WeChat Pay and Alipay.

    general advice
武

Training places

  • Training Monday to Friday, more than 6 hours a day. Times change with the seasons.

    confirmed

  • Main courtyard: basics, forms and Kung Fu. Meditation hall: Chan meditation and theory. Hills of the Chishuiwan scenic area: running and outdoor sessions.

    to be confirmed

  • Wear a comfortable T-shirt, trousers that do not limit your legs, long socks over the trousers and low-sole sports shoes.

    confirmed
网

Internet and phone

  • Wi-Fi in every room and in the common area.

    confirmed

  • Google, WhatsApp, Instagram and YouTube are blocked in mainland China: install a VPN before you arrive.

    general advice

  • A local SIM card can be bought in She Xian with your passport.

    to be confirmed

  • Add the school on WeChat: 15102661196.

    confirmed
医

Health and safety

  • Travel insurance is not mandatory but strongly recommended.

    confirmed

  • Emergency numbers in China: ambulance 120, police 110, fire 119.

    general advice

  • Tell a master if you are injured or unwell, even slightly.

    to be confirmed

  • Nearest pharmacy and clinic: in She Xian town.

    to be confirmed
规

Life at the school

  • In the Shaolin tradition, fellow students are brothers and sisters: help each other inside and outside training.

    confirmed

  • To leave the school for a trip, ask the masters' permission first.

    confirmed

  • Hot showers are available 24 hours. Air conditioning or heater in the room costs $100 per month.

    confirmed

  • Free time: ping-pong, basketball, Chinese chess, walks in the scenic area.

    confirmed
