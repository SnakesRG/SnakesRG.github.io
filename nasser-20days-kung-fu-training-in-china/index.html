Nasser Abu Sasha 20Days Kung Fu training in china

Nasser Abu Sasha. Country: Netherlands . Length of Training: 20 Days

This experience of studying Kung Fu in China has given me a deep sense of the depth of the Chinese martial arts and the uniqueness of the education system. I’ve tried several schools in China and finally found the best place for me to belong at the Shaolin Temple Cultural Center in China. This school has not only allowed me to improve my skills, but has also allowed me to experience the spirit of “cultivating one’s body with martial arts and educating people with culture”. I would like to share my feelings from various dimensions:

The location is excellent

live in the beautiful Chishuiwan Scenic Area, the beautiful scenic area provides a tranquil and peaceful living environment. You can get away from the hustle and bustle of the city and feel the quietness and peace of nature. with a smaller version of the Great Wall nearby, hot springs, and massage services. Plus, being inside the compound means we have access to numerous activities and shows for free, which others have to pay for. It truly feels like home. For me, it’s the best in China.”

Master’s rigorous teaching and humanistic care

I am impressed by the professionalism and dedication of the team. My master, Shi Yanhao, is a Shaolin martial arts monk who emphasizes the precision of the “horse stance like a pine, bow stance like a bow”, but also focuses on teaching students according to their aptitude. He will adjust the intensity of training according to the difference in physical ability of the students, and even use the time after school to guide the details of the movement. The encouragement and strict requirements of the masters “with a light in their eyes” are indispensable!

Perfect facilities and learning atmosphere: home-like sense of belonging

Training site: 200 square meters of practice area, a variety of training equipment to meet the needs of modern education
Life Support: Clean and cozy dormitories, nutritionally balanced meals in the cafeteria, and convenient and considerate school bus transportation services.
Community Interaction: Students help each other as a wind, and often organize their own after-school sparring routines, forming a “martial arts will be friends” atmosphere of healthy competition.

I can recommend this place to anyone.

I’m especially thankful to Master shi yanhao for putting so much faith and believe in me when I couldn’t myself sometimes.
